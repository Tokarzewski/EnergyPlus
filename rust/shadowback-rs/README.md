# shadowback-rs

Rust port of `shadowback.c`, bridged with the C ABI. Generated by rust-anything.

## Functions

- `ShadowBack` (`shadowback.c:8`)

## Loop

```sh
rust-anything record .   # record what the TS code does
rust-anything build .    # build the the C ABI module
rust-anything diff .     # run the recorded cases against Rust
rust-anything shadow .   # run both in the app, log mismatches
rust-anything gate .     # fmt, clippy, tests, unsafe, API, diff
```
## What is ported

`src/port.rs` ports a receiving surface with one window or door and up to two back surfaces,
shaded by one or two shadowing surfaces, as `src/EnergyPlus/SolarShading.cc` computes it:
`SHADOW` (the shadow projections and the receiving figure), `SHDGSS` with `MULTOL` (the general
shadows, as in unit 31), `SHDBKS` (each back surface the sun reaches through the receiving
surface: transformed to the receiving plane, projected along the sun onto it, its vertices
snapped to receiving vertices within 6 units, overlapped on the receiving figure in place and
kept with its surface number in `HCNS`), `SHDSBS` and `SHDRVL` (the subsurface and its reveal,
as in unit 32), then both clamps. `SHDBKS` compares the back surface's sun cosine with the
`Real64` `SunIsUpValue`, where `SHDGSS` truncates it to an `int`; its snapping compares in
`Real64` too. Back surfaces leave the sunlit areas as they are: their figures feed
`CalcInteriorSolarOverlaps`, which only windows that transmit reach.

The oracle (`rust/oracles/shadowback/`) gives the real `SHADOW` unit 32's scene plus surfaces 5
and 6 as the receiving surface's back surfaces (`ShadowComb.BackSurf`): the room's opposite
wall, floor, ceiling or side walls, an offset or smaller wall, a triangle, or a wall off to the
side, sometimes with vertices up to 0.08 mm off, and sun cosines that are mostly negative but
sometimes at or just past `-SunIsUpValue` or positive. `MaxHCS` is 20, more than the 16 figures
this needs, so `TooManyFigures` cannot arise. `FBKSHC` and `NBKSHC` are reset on every call, as
are unit 32's arrays. These return NaN in both (ADR-009): unit 32's guards, a back surface count
outside 0 to 2, and for the back surfaces in use vertex counts other than 3 or 4, vertices past
30 m and cosines past 1.

## Accepted gate exceptions (2026-11-23)

- **Size:** `rust-anything gate` fails only its size check: about 1330 lines in `src/port.rs`
  against a limit set by the 6-line C forwarder in `rust/oracles/shadowback/`. Waived under
  ADR-007.
- **Recording:** 12504 cases (200 scenes), fewer than unit 32's 28799. WSL went down every 15 to
  20 minutes while this unit was recorded, so the cases were recorded in chunks of 1500 with
  rust-anything's own C driver (`.rust-anything/driver_c.py record`) over the input list that
  `rust-anything record` had built, then joined in order; `recorded-from.json` comes from a
  normal `rust-anything record` run on the same source.
- **Speed:** 1.21x faster: 1575 ns per call against 1906 ns for the C++ (both include the
  oracle's setup). Timed by a direct in-process loop over the recorded cases, both sides
  called through a C function pointer, best of 15 interleaved runs, as for units 30 to 32.
  Slower than unit 32 (1.43x), probably because the larger figure table (`MaxHCS` 20) is zeroed
  on every call; not profiled.

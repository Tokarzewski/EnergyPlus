# shadowcast-rs

Rust port of `shadowcast.c`, bridged with the C ABI. Generated by rust-anything.

## Functions

- `ShadowCast` (`shadowcast.c:8`)

## Loop

```sh
rust-anything record .   # record what the TS code does
rust-anything build .    # build the the C ABI module
rust-anything diff .     # run the recorded cases against Rust
rust-anything shadow .   # run both in the app, log mismatches
rust-anything gate .     # fmt, clippy, tests, unsafe, API, diff
```

## What is ported

`src/port.rs` ports one receiving surface shaded by one shadowing surface, as
`src/EnergyPlus/SolarShading.cc` computes it: `SHADOW` (the shadow projections `XS / ZS` and
`YS / ZS` along the sun, the receiving surface brought into its own plane with `CTRANS`, reversed
to clockwise, `HTRANS1` and its area made positive) and `SHDGSS` for a single heat transfer surface
casting the shadow (`CTRANS` into the receiving plane, `CLIP` at that plane, the projection,
`HTRANS1`, the snap of each shadow vertex onto a receiving vertex within 6 units, `HTRANS0`,
`DeterminePolygonOverlap` written over the shadow figure, the `FirstSurfWithinSecond` adjustment
for a part-transparent shadow, and the sunlit area), then `SHADOW`'s clamp of the sunlit area to
[0, net area]. The overlap runs in place (`DeterminePolygonOverlap(1, NS3, NS3)`), so its sign test
reads the overlap's own area and a `TooManyVertices` overlap leaves the shadow figure as it was;
the port keeps both. The clipping is Sutherland-Hodgman or SlaterBarsky, from units 28 and 29.

The oracle (`rust/oracles/shadowcast/`) gives the real `SHADOW` a sunlit base surface 1 (the
receiving surface: world vertices, local axes, origin, net area) and a heat transfer surface 2
facing away from the sun (the caster: world vertices, schedule minimum) as its one general
shadowing surface, with no back surfaces or subsurfaces, so `SHDBKS` and `SHDSBS` do nothing.
These return NaN in both (ADR-009): an `algo` outside 0 or 1 (ConvexWeilerAtherton is left out:
its 10 m limit from unit 29 does not fit projected shadows); vertex counts other than 3 or 4;
vertices or origin past 30 m; local axis or sun components past 1; a schedule value outside
[0, 1] or an area outside [0, 1e6]; and a sun so low over the receiving plane that a projection
passes 4, which with the 30 m limit keeps every projected coordinate within the 1000 m where the
homogeneous products fit in `Int64`.

## Accepted gate exceptions (2026-11-22)

- **Size:** `rust-anything gate` fails only its size check: about 880 lines in `src/port.rs`
  against a limit set by the 6-line C forwarder in `rust/oracles/shadowcast/`. Waived under ADR-007.
- **Speed:** 1.86x faster: 334 ns per call against 621 ns for the C++ (both include the
  oracle's setup; the shim sizes and zeroes the figure arrays on every call).
  Timed by a direct in-process loop over the recorded cases, both sides called through a C
  function pointer, best of 15 interleaved runs: `rust-anything bench` ran past an hour while
  other sessions loaded the same WSL. The same loop gives 1.76x for unit 29 and 0.83x for
  unit 28, against 1.84x and 0.89x from `rust-anything bench`.

## What is ported

`src/port.rs` ports the optics helpers of `src/EnergyPlus/Material.cc`: `CalcScreenTransmittance`
(beam and scattered transmittance, reflectance and absorptance of a screen under all three beam
reflectance models), `GetRelativePhiTheta`, `NormalizePhiTheta`, `GetPhiThetaIndices`,
`GetProfIndices`, `GetSlatIndicesInterpFac` and `MaterialGlass::SetupSimpleWindowGlazingSystem`
(the simple glazing system's equivalent glass layer).

The oracle (`rust/oracles/matoptics/`) builds the screen and glass materials from plain numbers.
Its guards keep the routines in the inputs they are defined for, and both return NaN outside them
(ADR-009): `CalcScreenTransmittance` asserts its angles are in [0, pi]; `NormalizePhiTheta` loops
once per 2 pi, so angles past 1000 rad are refused; the index helpers convert to `int`. A simple
glazing whose conductivity comes out 0 or less (the C++'s fatal error) also returns NaN.

## Accepted gate exceptions (2026-11-22)

- **Size:** `rust-anything gate` fails only its size check: about 320 lines in `src/port.rs`
  against a limit set by the 36-line C forwarder in `rust/oracles/matoptics/`. Waived under ADR-007.
- **Speed:** 2.1x faster overall. Most of the 8.5x for `CalcScreenTransmittance` and the 4x for
  the simple glazing setup is the oracle constructing a full `MaterialScreen` or `MaterialGlass`
  per call; the helpers alone are 1.3x to 1.6x faster.

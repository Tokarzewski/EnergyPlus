# waterhc-rs

Rust port of `waterhc.c`, bridged with the C ABI. Generated by rust-anything.

## Functions

- `WaterHeatCoil` (`waterhc.c:8`)

## Loop

```sh
rust-anything record .   # record what the TS code does
rust-anything build .    # build the the C ABI module
rust-anything diff .     # run the recorded cases against Rust
rust-anything shadow .   # run both in the app, log mismatches
rust-anything gate .     # fmt, clippy, tests, unsafe, API, diff
```

## What is ported

`src/port.rs` ports `WaterCoils::CalcSimpleHeatingCoil` from `src/EnergyPlus/WaterCoils.cc`, which
EnergyPlus runs for every simple Coil:Heating:Water at every HVAC iteration, and many times per
iteration while a controller searches for the water flow:

- the cycling fan's air and water flows scaled by the part load ratio (the water flow limited to
  its maximum);
- the effectiveness-NTU model of a cross flow heat exchanger with both streams unmixed at a fixed
  UA, the outlet air and water temperatures, the heating rate and the outlet water enthalpy;
- the water specific heat from `fluidproperties-rs` (unit 2, with a one entry cache like the
  C++'s by temperature) and the air's and the outlet enthalpy from `psychrometrics-rs` (unit 1),
  both path dependencies.

The oracle (`rust/oracles/waterhc/`) sets up one coil as `InitWaterCoil` leaves it after sizing,
on a plant loop of water with the built-in fluid properties, then calls the real routine. These
return NaN in both (ADR-009): flags out of range, and any input that is not finite or lies outside
what EnergyPlus meets (see the shim; a UA of 0 is a fatal error, and the water specific heat warns
outside its table).

Not ported yet: the sizing and UA calculation passes (`MySizeFlag`, `MyUAAndFlowCalcFlag`), glycol
loops, the detailed and dehumidifying cooling coils, and `InitWaterCoil` and reporting.

## Accepted gate exceptions (2026-11-23)

- **Size:** `rust-anything gate` fails only its size check: 173 lines of Rust (with tests) against
  a limit set by the 6-line C forwarder in `rust/oracles/waterhc/`. Waived under ADR-007.
- **Recording, diff and gate:** 13811 cases in all: 2250 coils, each asked for all six results,
  plus rust-anything's generated cases. WSL kept going down and cutting long driver runs, so the
  cases were recorded and diffed in chunks of 1500 with the crate's own driver
  (`.rust-anything/driver_c.py record` and `diff`). All 13811/13811 match. The gate was run on a
  copy of this crate with the first 4000 cases: every check passes (behaviour 4000/4000) except
  size.
- **Speed:** slower than the C++, 0.85x: 56 ns per call against 48 ns. Both sides spend most of it
  in the same libm `pow` and `exp`. Without the specific heat cache the port was 0.74x, as the C++
  keeps its cache in the EnergyPlus state while the port's is thread local. Timed by a direct
  in-process loop over the recorded cases, both sides called through a C function pointer, best of
  15 interleaved runs.
